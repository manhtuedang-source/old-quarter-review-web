This walk is for you if…

  • You are in Hanoi on a weekend night
  • You like crowds, lights and street energy
  • You want the great market and the lake in one easy line
  • You prefer wandering with a map over following a tight schedule

Before you go

Carry water and small cash notes for stalls. Wear shoes you can walk in for two hours, expect uneven pavements, and keep your bag on the building side of the street. Every stop on this route can be skipped without breaking the walk, and cafe stops make natural rest breaks. Walking times are honest estimates, not promises, the quarter rewards wandering.
