Membership

Old Quarter Circle

A small membership for people planning a Hanoi trip, or already here (Old Quarter Circle is a working name). Every question is answered by Suzy herself, in a private chat, and the first group is limited to 30 members. That limit is Suzy's answering capacity, stated plainly: one person answers everything, so the group stays small enough to answer well.

Ask Suzy, one to one

Members ask questions in a private 1:1 chat on WhatsApp Business. Answers come within 24 to 48 hours, from Suzy herself, a real person, not a chatbot. One to one means your number stays yours: members never see each other's numbers.

Order a plan

Ask for a route, or a shortlist of places, built around your trip and your tastes. This is advice only: you make your own bookings, and guided walks are booked with Bao Son Travel, who sell and operate the tours.

Request a review early

Members can ask Suzy to review a place before it is filmed for the channel. Reviews made this way are published with the label Requested by a member, and Suzy pays her own bill at the venue.

Questions, answered straight

Who answers my questions?

Suzy herself. A real person, not a chatbot, and not an assistant writing in her name. If she is filming, your answer may take the full 24 to 48 hours; it still comes from her.

How do I cancel?

Cancel on Gumroad at any time, from your Gumroad account. There is no lock in. Your membership stays active until the end of the month you have paid for, and renewals simply stop.

What about refunds?

Refunds follow the refund policy. The short version: time paid for but not used is refunded, and if we cancel something, you choose a new time or your money back.

Can a venue pay to be reviewed kindly?

No. If a venue ever pays for coverage, that coverage is not a review. It is labelled Sponsored, and payment never changes what a review says. The full draft is public: the requested review policy.
