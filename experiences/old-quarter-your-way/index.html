Why I created this one

Some travellers arrive with a list. Others arrive with a feeling: old houses, street food at dawn, photography, one perfect gift to find. Your Way starts from that feeling instead of a fixed route. Tell us the theme and the pace, and Suzy drafts a map from the streets she films every week. You adjust it until it sounds like your day. Bao Son Travel then quotes it in writing, and a licensed guide leads it, with Suzy joining when her schedule allows. Nothing on this route is copied from a catalogue, because it begins with you.

Honest answers, including the limits

What does it cost?

There is no catalogue price because there is no catalogue route. Once your draft map is agreed, Bao Son Travel sends one written, line by line quote. You pay the company, never a person, and never in chat.

How much notice do you need?

Two days or more lets us draft a thoughtful route and confirm your guide. Shorter notice is sometimes possible in the pilot season; ask and we will answer honestly within 12 hours.

Can I ask for anything?

Almost. If a wish is not practical, not safe, or not allowed, for example entering the train street lanes at barrier times, we will say so plainly and offer the closest honest alternative.

Will Suzy walk with us?

Suzy designs every Your Way route and joins when her schedule allows. A licensed guide leads your day either way, and the route notes carry her picks and warnings.
