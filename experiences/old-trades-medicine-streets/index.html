Why I created this one

Most visitors walk the same ten streets. This is the walk I give friends who stay longer: the working quarter of herbs, tin and fabric, where the guild system is still a living economy. It is slower, quieter and, to me, the most honest part of the Old Quarter.

The quarter tourists miss: herb shops of Lan Ong, tin and copper hammering, fabric and rattan. Slow, curious and uncrowded, this is the working Old Quarter of today.

Honest answers, including the limits

Is this route touristy?

Honestly, no, and that is the point. These are working streets where workshops serve Hanoi first. Expect real trade, fewer souvenirs, and time to watch.

Can I photograph inside the workshops?

Always ask first. Your guide will ask the owner for you and translate; most makers are proud to be filmed once they are asked properly.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain is fine, most stops are under cover. If weather makes walking unsafe, Bao Son Travel rearranges the date with you in writing before the day.
