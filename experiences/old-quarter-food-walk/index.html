Why I created this one

I built this walk because visitors kept asking me the same question: where do Hanoians actually eat at night? Not the famous places with the longest tourist queues, but the stalls my family returns to. This route is my honest answer, four streets of grazing where every stop earns its place by taste and fair price.

Graze the east side with a licensed local guide: bia hoi corner, grill smoke, noodle steam and the dessert carts, at the pace of your appetite. Food stops are chosen for taste and honesty of price, and the route adjusts to your dietary needs.

Honest answers, including the limits

What happens if it rains?

Light rain does not stop the walk, the stalls cook under awnings and the quarter glows. If weather makes walking unsafe, Bao Son Travel rearranges the date with you in writing before the day.

Can we bring a stroller?

Honestly, we advise against it. Old Quarter pavements are narrow and uneven, and a baby carrier is far easier than a stroller on this route.

I have a serious allergy. Is this safe for me?

Tell us in your inquiry, exactly what the allergy is. Your guide briefs every stop before you order, but street kitchens share woks and oil, so for severe allergies we will say plainly if a stop is not safe, and always carry your own medication.

How far do we actually walk?

About 1 kilometre in total, in short hops between stops, with sitting time at every tasting. Nobody has ever finished this walk tired, only full.
