Old Quarter Food Walk

▶The film for this experience is in production. This is our illustration of its streets.

Why I created this one

I built this walk because visitors kept asking me the same question: where do Hanoians actually eat at night? Not the famous places with the longest tourist queues, but the stalls my family returns to. This route is my honest answer, four streets of grazing where every stop earns its place by taste and fair price.

Graze the east side with a licensed local guide: bia hoi corner, grill smoke, noodle steam and the dessert carts, at the pace of your appetite. Food stops are chosen for taste and honesty of price, and the route adjusts to your dietary needs.

A sample day, hour by hour

This is a sample plan, not a promise: your guide adjusts the order and the stops to the day, the weather and your appetite.

5:30 PM
Meet on Hang Be

Start hungry. Your guide checks dietary needs and spice limits before the first bite.

5:45 PM
Grills of Hang Buom

The food street proper. Small plates, big smoke, prices shown before you order.

6:45 PM
Noodle steam on Ma May

A steaming bowl where the old merchant houses glow. Slower pace, time to sit.

7:30 PM
Dessert carts

Sweet soups and soft cakes from the carts locals queue at after dinner.

8:00 PM
Bia hoi finish on Ta Hien

End at the famous beer corner with a glass of fresh bia hoi, the proper Hanoi goodnight.
