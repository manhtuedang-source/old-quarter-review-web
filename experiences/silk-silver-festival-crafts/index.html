Why I created this one

The guild streets are the reason this channel exists. Every street here was one craft, and on weekday mornings you can still hear and see the work happening. I designed this route for travellers who collect skills and stories, not just photos.

Watch the guild streets work: silk measured on Hang Gai, silver weighed on Hang Bac, lantern colour on Hang Ma. A walk for people who love how things are made, with time to buy directly from the makers.

This experience is for you if…

  • You love watching skilled people make real things
  • You would rather buy from the maker than from a mall
  • You want to tell real silk and real silver from the copies
  • You enjoy slow mornings and working streets more than big sights

A sample day, hour by hour

This is a sample plan, not a promise: your guide adjusts the order and the stops to the day, the weather and your appetite.

8:00 AM
Colour on Hang Ma

Lanterns and festival decor while the street is calm and the light is kind.

8:45 AM
Silversmiths of Hang Bac

Tiny hammers, real silver, and how to tell craft from casting.

9:45 AM
Coffee pause

A slow egg coffee, and the story of the family that invented it.

10:15 AM
Silk on Hang Gai

Feel real silk against the copies, and learn the burn test before you buy.

11:30 AM
Finish on Hang Bong

Bedding, tailoring and one last look at the quarter waking up.

Honest answers, including the limits

Do I have to buy anything?

Never. Browsing is half the pleasure of these streets, buying is always your choice, and prices are each shop's own. Your guide will happily carry on walking.

What happens if it rains?

Most of this route runs under shop awnings, so light rain is fine. If weather makes walking unsafe, Bao Son Travel rearranges the date with you in writing before the day.

Is the pace suitable for older travellers?

The route is flat and the pace is browsing pace, with a seated coffee pause in the middle. Tell us about mobility needs in your inquiry and the guide will plan extra rests.
