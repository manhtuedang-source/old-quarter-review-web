Why I created this one

Not everyone needs a guided day. Some travellers just need a local to look at their plan before they land and say: this is too much, this is a tourist trap, this tiny street is the one you will remember. That is exactly what this call is for.

Bring your dates and your wishlist. Suzy reviews your plan with you, marks the map, and tells you plainly what to skip. Written summary after the call, bookings only when you ask for them.

This experience is for you if…

  • You are planning your own Hanoi days and want a local second opinion
  • Your draft itinerary feels too full and you do not know what to cut
  • You land soon and want one honest review before you arrive
  • You prefer honest local advice over another generic top ten list

Honest answers, including the limits

What should I send before the call?

Your dates, your draft plan in any shape, and your questions. The rougher the draft, the more useful the call, please do not polish it for us.

Will you try to sell me tours on the call?

No. The call is advice only. If you later want a booking, it goes through Bao Son Travel with a written quote, only when you ask for it.

What if I need to move the call?

Message us and we will find a new time together. Time zones are our problem to solve, not yours.

This experience is sold and operated by Bao Son Travel, a licensed travel company, and led by a licensed guide. Suzy designs the route and joins as your host when her schedule allows.
