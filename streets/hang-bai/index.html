Lake & South Gate

Hàng Bài

Original trade: Playing cards and paper goods. A calm street running south from the lake, historically tied to card and paper trades.

Like every street in this quarter, Hàng Bài began as a guild street of old Thang Long, where one craft or one trade gathered on one street and gave the street its name. Our full street story, filmed on location with Suzy, is in production for the channel.
