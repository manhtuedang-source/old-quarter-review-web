Dong Xuan & North Gate

Đồng Xuân

Original trade: The great covered market. Hanoi's largest indoor market, three floors of wholesale and retail since the French era.

Like every street in this quarter, Đồng Xuân began as a guild street of old Thang Long, where one craft or one trade gathered on one street and gave the street its name. Our full street story, filmed on location with Suzy, is in production for the channel.
