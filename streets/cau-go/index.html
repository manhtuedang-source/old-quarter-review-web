Lake & South Gate

Cầu Gỗ

Original trade: The old wooden bridge crossing. Named after a wooden bridge that once crossed the stream feeding the lake.

Like every street in this quarter, Cầu Gỗ began as a guild street of old Thang Long, where one craft or one trade gathered on one street and gave the street its name. Our full street story, filmed on location with Suzy, is in production for the channel.
