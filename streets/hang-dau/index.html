Dong Xuan & North Gate

Hàng Đậu

Original trade: Bean trading. A northern street of bean and dry goods traders beneath the Long Bien bridge approach.

Like every street in this quarter, Hàng Đậu began as a guild street of old Thang Long, where one craft or one trade gathered on one street and gave the street its name. Our full street story, filmed on location with Suzy, is in production for the channel.
