Old Trades Centre West

Hàng Thùng

Original trade: Lacquered bamboo buckets. Once makers of lacquered bamboo buckets, now mixed local trade.

Like every street in this quarter, Hàng Thùng began as a guild street of old Thang Long, where one craft or one trade gathered on one street and gave the street its name. Our full street story, filmed on location with Suzy, is in production for the channel.
