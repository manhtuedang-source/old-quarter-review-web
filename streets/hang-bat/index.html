Old Trades Centre West

Hàng Bát

Original trade: Bowls and dishes. The bowl and dish street, today also groceries and household goods.

Like every street in this quarter, Hàng Bát began as a guild street of old Thang Long, where one craft or one trade gathered on one street and gave the street its name. Our full street story, filmed on location with Suzy, is in production for the channel.
