Train Street & West Edge

Trần Phú

Original trade: Train street cafe section. The cafe lined train street section, visit only as current access rules allow.

Like every street in this quarter, Trần Phú began as a guild street of old Thang Long, where one craft or one trade gathered on one street and gave the street its name. Our full street story, filmed on location with Suzy, is in production for the channel.
