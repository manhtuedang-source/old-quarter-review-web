Silver & Festival Crafts

Hàng Rươi

Original trade: Seasonal sand worm delicacy. A short street famous in season for cha ruoi, the autumn sand worm omelette.

Like every street in this quarter, Hàng Rươi began as a guild street of old Thang Long, where one craft or one trade gathered on one street and gave the street its name. Our full street story, filmed on location with Suzy, is in production for the channel.
