Dong Xuan & North Gate

Cầu Đông

Original trade: Market bridge street. A busy trading street feeding into Dong Xuan Market from the east.

Like every street in this quarter, Cầu Đông began as a guild street of old Thang Long, where one craft or one trade gathered on one street and gave the street its name. Our full street story, filmed on location with Thu, is in production for the channel.
