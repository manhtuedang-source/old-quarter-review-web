Arrival day

Noi Bai airport to the Old Quarter, step by step

Noi Bai (HAN) sits about 45 minutes north of the Old Quarter. The airport itself is straightforward. The stress points are the arrivals hall touts and the ride into town, and both disappear once you know the steps and the fair prices.

From the plane to the street

  1. Immigration. Follow the signs to passport control. Have your passport and your e-Visa copy ready. Exempt visitors go through the same desks. Queues move faster than they look.
  2. Baggage. Belts are one floor down after immigration. Porters and SIM sellers may approach you here; a polite no is a complete sentence.
  3. Customs and out. Most visitors walk through the green channel. The doors open into the arrivals hall, which is where the noise starts.
  4. Money and data, briefly. Change only a small amount at the airport counters, their rates are noticeably worse than in town, or use an ATM. For data, an eSIM set up before you fly beats the airport SIM counters on price; see our eSIM and SIM guide.
  5. Choose your ride below, then leave. Ignore anyone inside the hall offering a taxi, a SIM or a tour. Official options are all outside or inside your apps.

The ride to the Old Quarter, fair prices

OptionFair priceGood to know
Bus 8645,000 VND, official fareThe budget winner. Runs from both terminals to the city with stops near the Old Quarter, takes 60 to 80 minutes with luggage space. Pay the conductor, keep small notes ready.
Grab carAbout 250,000 to 350,000 VNDThe price in the app before you confirm is the price you pay. Walk to the app pickup point and match the plate, never the driver who finds you first.
Metered taxiSimilar range to Grab when the meter runsUse the official taxi queue outside arrivals. The meter must be on before the car moves; if it stays off, get out and take the next car.

The three arrivals hall rules

One, nobody legitimate sells tours, SIMs or rides by walking up to you indoors. Two, a price agreed per person, per 10 minutes or by a card shown at the end is a trap being set; agree one total price, in writing when you can. Three, if a dispute starts anyway, stay calm and call the tourism hotline 1800 599 920, or the police on 113. Our emergency card keeps every number on one page.

Going back to the airport

Leave the Old Quarter 3 hours before an international flight and 2 hours before a domestic one, more in rush hour or rain. Bus 86 runs back to Noi Bai from the city, and the same Grab and prebooked options apply. While you are here, the visa and entry page covers the paperwork side of the trip.
