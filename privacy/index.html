Privacy, in plain words

This website is a static guide. It has no accounts, no booking forms and no comment system, and it does not collect your personal data.

If you message the channel on WhatsApp or Messenger, your conversation stays inside those apps under their own privacy policies. We use it only to answer you and, when you ask for a tour, to pass your dates and group size to Bao Son Travel so they can quote and book for you.

We count visits with cookie free page counts by Cloudflare Web Analytics: no cookies, no advertising trackers and no personal data collected. Contact clicks pass through short counting pages on this site so we can see which experiences people ask about; the message you write stays in your own browser and is never stored on a server. We never sell contact details, and we never share your messages with advertisers.

When online booking opens in a later phase, this page will be updated before the first booking is taken, with the name of the company that processes your data and how to reach it.
