Evening honestly: three things to know

The night market runs on weekend evenings and the spine streets get dense; keep bags zipped and phones pocketed at the crossings. Bia hoi corners are cash places with small stools and big noise, which is the point, and they wind down earlier than visitors expect. And the lake after dark belongs to Hanoians exercising and courting; join the walk, it is the calmest hour of the day.
