Silver, Paper and Festival Crafts

Some streets change with the festival calendar, and this cluster leads them. Hang Ma fills with paper offerings, lanterns and decorations that turn red and gold before Tet and the Mid Autumn Festival. A few steps away, Hang Bac is where Hanoi's silver trade began, with jewellers still working behind small counters, and Hang Luoc sells the combs that once completed every wedding trousseau.

The streets of this area

Numbered in walking order. Tap a street for its full story.

1Silver and jewelleryHàng Bạc

The original silversmiths' street, still lined with jewellers and gold and silver shops.

2Votive paper and festival decorHàng Mã

The most colourful street in the quarter before Tet and Mid Autumn, full of lanterns and paper goods.

3CombsHàng Lược

The comb sellers' street, once supplying wooden and horn combs to the whole city.

4Seasonal sand worm delicacyHàng Rươi

A short street famous in season for cha ruoi, the autumn sand worm omelette.

5Bottle trading, now local eateriesHàng Chai

A narrow street near the market, today loved for small local food stalls.
