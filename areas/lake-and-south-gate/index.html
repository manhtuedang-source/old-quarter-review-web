The Lake and the South Gate

Every first trip to Hanoi starts here. Hoan Kiem Lake is the compass of the Old Quarter, and the streets around its southern edge hold the postcard views: the Turtle Tower at golden hour, the red bridge to Ngoc Son Temple, the water puppet theatre and the French era facades of Trang Tien. Come at dawn to share the lake shore with tai chi groups, or at sunset when the whole city seems to slow down for one lap around the water.

The streets of this area

Numbered in walking order. Tap a street for its full story.

1Lacquered trays and boxesHàng Khay

One of the short streets along the lake, once known for lacquerware trays and betel boxes.

2Playing cards and paper goodsHàng Bài

A calm street running south from the lake, historically tied to card and paper trades.

3Folk paintings and drumsHàng Trống

Home of the Hang Trong folk painting tradition, printed by hand on paper for Tet and festivals.

4Luggage and travel goodsHàng Hành

A small street of bag and luggage shops close to the lake and the Cathedral.

5The old wooden bridge crossingCầu Gỗ

Named after a wooden bridge that once crossed the stream feeding the lake.
