One quarter, eight areas, every guild street

The famous number 36 began as the 36 guild wards of fifteenth century Thang Long. Today the Old Quarter has around 76 streets. We group the iconic ones into eight walkable areas. Tap an area to open its streets.

Staying near the quarter

Our basecamp is the quiet side of the centre: the Van Mieu neighbourhood by the Temple of Literature, about a ten minute ride from the lake. Close enough for dawn walks in the quarter, calm enough for a full night of sleep.
