Hanoi seasons, the short honest version

November to February: cool and dry, the kindest walking months, with a real chill in January. March to April: warming up, drizzle and flower streets. May to September: hot, humid, with fast afternoon rain; walk early and rest at noon. October: the golden month, milk flower scent and the clearest skies of the year.
