1. Message the channel on WhatsApp or Messenger with your dates, your group size and what you love. Tell us any dietary needs (vegetarian, halal, allergies), spice limits, kids' ages and how fast you like to walk, so the plan fits you from the start.

Questions visitors ask us

I am vegetarian. Will I eat well?

Yes. Tell us when you write, and the route is planned around chay (vegetarian) kitchens and dishes, with allergen and spice notes for every stop.

We have young kids. Is it suitable?

Morning and early evening walks suit families best. Routes can be shortened, rest stops added, and the spiciest dishes swapped out.

How and when do I pay?

Only after you accept the written quote, and always to Bao Son Travel directly, never to a person in chat. The quote lists what is included, line by line.

What if it rains?

Light rain rarely stops a walk in Hanoi, and ponchos are easy to find. If the weather turns severe, the company will agree a new time with you under the terms in your written quote.

Is Suzy our guide?

A licensed guide leads your day, as the law requires. Suzy designs the routes and joins as your host when her schedule allows.

How fast do we walk?

At your pace. Routes are short, stops are frequent, and any stop can be skipped. Tell us about mobility needs when you write and the plan will adapt.
