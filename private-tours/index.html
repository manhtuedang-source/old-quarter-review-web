Private tours, designed by a local

The videos are free forever. If they make you want a private walk, here is how it works, plainly and legally.

Old Quarter Food Walk

About 3 hours, evening

Graze the east side with a licensed local guide: bia hoi corner, grill smoke, noodle steam and the dessert carts, at the pace of your appetite. Food stops are chosen for taste and honesty of price, and the route adjusts to your dietary needs.

Silk, Silver and Festival Crafts

About 4 hours, morning

Watch the guild streets work: silk measured on Hang Gai, silver weighed on Hang Bac, lantern colour on Hang Ma. A walk for people who love how things are made, with time to buy directly from the makers.

Old Trades and Medicine Streets

About 3 hours, morning

The quarter tourists miss: herb shops of Lan Ong, tin and copper hammering, fabric and rattan. Slow, curious and uncrowded, this is the working Old Quarter of today.

Plan My Hanoi, a personal consult

Video call, about 45 minutes

Bring your dates and your wishlist. Thu reviews your plan with you, marks the map, and tells you plainly what to skip. Written summary after the call, bookings only when you ask for them.

How booking works

1. Message the channel on WhatsApp or Messenger with your dates, your group size and what you love. Tell us any dietary needs (vegetarian, halal, allergies), spice limits, kids' ages and how fast you like to walk, so the plan fits you from the start.

2. Bao Son Travel, a licensed travel company, replies with a plan and a written price with everything included.

3. You confirm and pay the company directly. A licensed guide leads your day, and Thu joins as your host when the schedule allows.

This page and this channel never take bookings or payments in chat, and never quote prices. Prices live in the written quote from the company, so they can be checked line by line.

Questions visitors ask us

I am vegetarian. Will I eat well?

Yes. Tell us when you write, and the route is planned around chay (vegetarian) kitchens and dishes, with allergen and spice notes for every stop.

We have young kids. Is it suitable?

Morning and early evening walks suit families best. Routes can be shortened, rest stops added, and the spiciest dishes swapped out.

How and when do I pay?

Only after you accept the written quote, and always to Bao Son Travel directly, never to a person in chat. The quote lists what is included, line by line.

What if it rains?

Light rain rarely stops a walk in Hanoi, and ponchos are easy to find. If the weather turns severe, the company will agree a new time with you under the terms in your written quote.

Is Thu our guide?

A licensed guide leads your day, as the law requires. Thu designs the routes and joins as your host when her schedule allows.

How fast do we walk?

At your pace. Routes are short, stops are frequent, and any stop can be skipped. Tell us about mobility needs when you write and the plan will adapt.
