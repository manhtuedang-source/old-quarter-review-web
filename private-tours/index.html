Private tours, designed by a local

The videos are free forever. If they make you want a private walk, here is how it works, plainly and legally.

Old Quarter Food Walk

About 3 hours, evening

Graze the east side with a licensed local guide: bia hoi corner, grill smoke, noodle steam and the dessert carts, at the pace of your appetite. Food stops are chosen for taste and honesty of price, and the route adjusts to your dietary needs.

Silk, Silver and Festival Crafts

About 4 hours, morning

Watch the guild streets work: silk measured on Hang Gai, silver weighed on Hang Bac, lantern colour on Hang Ma. A walk for people who love how things are made, with time to buy directly from the makers.

Old Trades and Medicine Streets

About 3 hours, morning

The quarter tourists miss: herb shops of Lan Ong, tin and copper hammering, fabric and rattan. Slow, curious and uncrowded, this is the working Old Quarter of today.

Plan My Hanoi, a personal consult

Video call, about 45 minutes

Bring your dates and your wishlist. Thu reviews your plan with you, marks the map, and tells you plainly what to skip. Written summary after the call, bookings only when you ask for them.
