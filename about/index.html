About Suzy, your host

I am Suzy, your host at Old Quarter Review. I live and work in Hanoi, and the Old Quarter is the part of my city I never get tired of.

On this channel I speak English on camera and take you where my own family eats, drinks coffee and buys gifts. You will see real portions, real prices and the small details guidebooks skip, from egg coffee at dawn to the night market on a Friday.

One honest note on how I work: this channel is my personal project. I am not a licensed tour guide yet, and I never take bookings or payments in chat. If a video makes you want a private tour, I connect you with the licensed travel team who sells and runs it, and a licensed guide leads your day.

Disclosure: my family works for Bao Son Group, the company that owns Hanoi Larosa Hotel. When I show a place connected to the group, I will always tell you. Reviews on this channel are never paid and never staged.
