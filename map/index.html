The Lake and the South Gate

Hoan Kiem Lake, the Cathedral and sunset walks

Every first trip to Hanoi starts here. Hoan Kiem Lake is the compass of the Old Quarter, and the streets around its southern edge hold the postcard views: the Turtle Tower at golden hour, the red bridge to Ngoc Son Temple, the water puppet theatre and the French era facades of Trang Tien. Come at dawn to share the lake shore with tai chi groups, or at sunset when the whole city seems to slow down for one lap around the water.
